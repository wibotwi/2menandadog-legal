Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Who we are

Fit Daddy ("the app") is made by 2 Men and a Dog, based in Uruguay. We are responsible for the personal data described here.

Questions or requests about your data: twomenandadogco@gmail.com

What we collect

Account: your email address, name and profile photo, if you choose one. Your password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form; we can never see it.

Health data, only if you allow it in Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone): daily steps, weight measurements, and food you logged in other apps (what you ate, when, and its nutrients).

Goals and settings: your targets (such as steps, calories and protein), language, units and other preferences.

What you and your coach create in the app: for example messages, photos and files, notes, tasks, schedules and reading material.

Technical data: app version, the phone's operating system, maker, model, language and time zone; records of app activity and errors; and feedback you send us. Technical data never contains health values such as your steps, weight or food.

How we use it

To show you your data, goals and progress, and to let your coach see your data and work with you.

To send you the notifications you allowed.

To keep the service secure, and to find and fix problems.

We do not show ads, do not sell or rent your data, do not use it for advertising or profiling, do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools, and do not use your health data to train AI models.

Health Connect and Apple Health

The app reads steps, weight and nutrition from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone), only with your permission. You can turn this off at any time in the app (Profile → Connected services) or in the phone's health settings.

With your permission, the app also reads new data while it is closed, so your coach sees up-to-date data. You can turn this off in the app.

The use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone except your coach.

Who can see your data

You.

Your coach, only while you are linked: your name, photo, goals and health data, and what you create together. Coaches can only view your health data, never change it. You link by entering their invite code and can unlink at any time. If you are a coach, your students see your name, photo and what you share with them.

Service providers that run the app for us: our hosting provider (stores the data), and Google or Apple (deliver notifications to your phone). They only process the data to provide these services.

Content from other services: some content in the app, such as videos, is provided by other services (for example YouTube). When you open or play it, that service receives your IP address and device information, under its own privacy policy.

No one else, except if required by law.

Where your data is stored

On your phone (a copy, so the app works quickly and offline), and on servers in the United States. By using the app, your data is transferred to and stored in the United States.

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access rules make sure that only you and your coach can read your data.

How long we keep it

We keep your data as long as your account exists, including the history of your health data.

Signing out only removes the copy on that phone. Your data stays on our servers until you delete your account (see "Your choices and rights" below).

Technical records are deleted after at most 180 days. Feedback is kept until we have dealt with it, at the latest until your account is deleted.

What you and a coach created together is deleted when you unlink from that coach.

When your account is deleted, we delete everything listed above, except a log of account activity that we keep for security and accountability. It contains no health data, but it does contain email addresses and device details.

Your choices and rights

You can see and change your name, photo, goals and settings in the app, choose which notifications you get, turn health access off, and unlink from your coach at any time.

To get a copy of your data or to have it corrected, email us.

To delete your account and data: https://wibotwi.github.io/2menandadog-legal/delete-account.html (also in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account).

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. For important changes we will tell you in the app.

Больше никто, кроме случаев, предусмотренных законом.

На вашем телефоне (копия, чтобы приложение работало быстро и без интернета) и на серверах в США. Пользуясь приложением, вы соглашаетесь с передачей и хранением ваших данных в США.
